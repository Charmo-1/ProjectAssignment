Physical Priorities

While you do not need elite physical fitness on day one, focusing your personal workouts on these specific athletic dimensions will drastically accelerate your adaptation to heavy steel armor combat.

Cardiovascular Endurance: Focus on high-intensity interval training (HIIT) to mimic the oxygen deprived conditions of fighting inside a sealed helmet.

A Knight's Progression

Phase 1: Finding a proper Team

Do not buy steel armor online before attending a practice. Your first critical step is locating a regional team. Use the official AMCF Club Locator to find regional groups near you. Working under an official team ensures you learn correct form and prevent expensive sizing mistakes. You'll also get the right experience to allow you to compete.

Phase 2: Conditioning and Soft Kit Training

Initial training relies entirely on soft gear, including padded armor sets, high-impact foam shields, and lightweight plastic/foam weapons. This environment allows you to develop stance mechanics, striking patterns, grappling control safely, and the most important, proper teamwork.

Phase 3: Tailoring Your Steel Armor

Once your footwork and endurance satisfy regional marshals, you will take precise body dimensions under your team veteran's supervision. Every plate component must be custom fit to your exact physique to distribute the massive weight safely. Visit the Arms & Armor page to see where to get started.

Cross the Barrier

No Prior Experience Required

Athletes enter Buhurt from all backgrounds, including powerlifting, rugby, traditional martial arts, and historical reenactments. The only true prerequisites are absolute mental discipline, a high work ethic, and an eagerness to learn complex mechanical combat mechanics.
